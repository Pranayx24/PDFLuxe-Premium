PDFLUXE PREMIUM

Privacy

PDFLuxe Premium is designed around a simple principle: your documents should remain yours.

Local-first processing

PDFLuxe Premium is designed to process supported PDF operations directly in your browser whenever possible.

Your selected documents are not intentionally uploaded to a PDFLuxe server for the core browser-based workflows.

Files you select

Files selected through the PDF workspace are handled by the browser and the local JavaScript processing environment.

When you download a processed document, the resulting file is generated on your device.

No account required

The core PDF workspace does not require you to create an account before using the browser-based tools.

Third-party services

PDFLuxe Premium may use third-party libraries or services required for application functionality, such as PDF processing libraries and web fonts.

Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Important limitation

PDFLuxe Premium is an evolving project. This page describes the intended privacy model of the browser-based application and is not a legal guarantee for future versions or third-party integrations.

Contact

For questions about PDFLuxe Premium, use the project's GitHub repository.
